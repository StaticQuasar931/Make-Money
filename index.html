Prestige

Prestiging gives you a permanent income boost. When you prestige, all your progress will be reset including upgrades and bonus clicks. However, you will get (your number of items - 10) prestige points. Each prestige point gives a +50% income boost and does not get reset when you prestige again! Prestiging is very useful if you are stuck and cannot make any progress. This way, you can prestige and gain income boost and get back there faster and make more progress.

Basics

Make Money is a simple clicking game in which you try to earn as much money as possible. Click the earn button and gain an amount of money equal to your income.
Navigation

Make Money is split into several main areas. The Money tab is the main tab in which you can earn money. The Income tab allows you to view all of your possessions. The Upgrades tab contains upgrades to improve your income and items. The Help tab is for help. The Settings area allows you to adjust the options of the game. The Prestige area is for prestiging to gain prestige points. The Casino tab is for gambling money.

Casino

In the Casino, you can gamble your money to try and win more. Enter the amount you wish to bet and click "GAMBLE". The slot machine will spin, and based on the outcome, you can win or lose money. Be careful, as gambling can be risky!
